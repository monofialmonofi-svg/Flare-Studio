## Conclusion on sourcing
- The **defection** story traces to **one or two origins** (Sudanese Echo, and Al-Hadath per reports). Dozens of outlets repeated it, but **repetition is not corroboration**.
- The **denial** has two on-record sources: the RSF media office and **Bireish himself on video**.
- **No independent party** (the SAF, a neutral body or an international organisation) confirmed the defection as of 11 Nov 2026.
