**Subject:** Lt. Gen. Khalid Nour al-Daim Bireish (الفريق خالد نور الدائم بريش), RSF
**Checked up to:** 11 Nov 2026
**Method:** web search across Arabic and English outlets. Direct pages of news sites, Facebook, X and YouTube are blocked in this environment, so content was read through search results and syndicated copies.

## Status key
| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ CONFIRMED | Several independent outlets report the same fact, or it is an on-record statement by the party itself |
| 🟡 REPORTED | Reported by media, traced to a single original source, not independently confirmed |
| 🔴 DENIED | Explicitly denied by the party concerned |
| ⚪ NOT FOUND | Requested in the brief, but no source could be found; **must not appear as fact** |

## 2. Context (verified, optional on screen)
- 2 Nov 2026: the SAF announced that a group of RSF members defected and surrendered to its 4th Infantry Division in Damazin ([AA](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/africa/sudanese-army-says-rsf-members-defect-in-blue-nile-state/4108670)).
- Defection claims and denials have repeatedly been part of the media war between the two sides (noted in several reports). This is why claim 1 alone does not prove a defection.

- No military movements, locations or operations (claim 2) are used.
