## The ICC case ([ICC case page](https://www.icc-cpi.int/darfur/harun), [case information sheet](https://www.icc-cpi.int/sites/default/files/CaseInformationSheets/haruneng.pdf))
- Arrest warrant issued **27 April 2007** by Pre-Trial Chamber I.
- **42 counts:** 20 crimes against humanity and 22 war crimes, relating to Darfur, mainly 2003–2004.
- His position when the warrant was issued: Minister of State for the Interior.
- The ICC page lists him as **at large**. There is **no** official announcement of his transfer to the ICC; the video must not say or imply one.
- Editorial note for the screen: **accusations are not a conviction.**
- Source discrepancy: a 2008 Security Council report mentions 51 counts. That figure appears to combine his warrant with Ali Kushayb's, so we use the ICC's own number (42).
