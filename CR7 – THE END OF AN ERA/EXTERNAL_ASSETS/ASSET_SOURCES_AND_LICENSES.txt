PLUGINS / UTILITIES  (EXTERNAL_ASSETS/PLUGINS)
 No plugins downloaded. FFmpeg (pre-installed) plus numpy, scipy, Pillow and soundfile (from pypi.org) were used.
 The final cut contains NO voiceover, narration or synthetic speech. The text-to-speech voice model and the
 speech-recognition model used in earlier drafts were removed from the project.

FOOTAGE / MUSIC / SOUND_EFFECTS / MOTION_GRAPHICS / LUTS — no external assets downloaded:
 - Wikimedia Commons has no freely licensed Ronaldo/Portugal match video or usable stadium footage (searched 2026-11-05).
 - mixkit.co and pixabay.com (free-licence stock video/music/SFX) answer with a Cloudflare human-verification (CAPTCHA)
   challenge to this environment; per instructions I stopped there and did not attempt to bypass it.
 - Official licensed match footage (UEFA/FIFA/FPF) requires a paid licence — needs your authorisation/purchase.
 Instead, all music, sound effects, motion graphics (light streaks, particles, light rays, bokeh) and the colour grade are
 ORIGINAL, generated by code in Project_Files/scripts — no third-party rights involved.
